I am a Ph.D. researcher in **Electrical and Computer Engineering** at **Purdue University**, advised by [Prof. Kaushik Roy](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=to4P8KgAAAAJ) in the **Nano(electronic) Research Laboratory**. My work develops **training-free mathematical frameworks** for efficient and trustworthy foundation models — no finetuning, no extra data, no architectural changes.

My research spans three interlocking threads:

- **Diffusion model compression** — closed-form, activation-aware structural slimming of Stable Diffusion and Flux through SVD, importance-guided rank allocation, and trajectory-aware calibration ([SlimDiff](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.21498)).
- **Local learning beyond backprop** — structured low-rank feedback alignment on manifolds, scaling Direct Feedback Alignment to modern architectures (*SSA*, WACV 2026 Highlight).
- **Machine unlearning** — subspace-suppression methods for safe concept erasure in generative models and label-noise correction in discriminative ones ([ICE](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.18684), [CURE](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.12677)).

I work on the **Gautschi HPC cluster** and have served as a reviewer for **NeurIPS, ICLR, CVPR, ICCV, WACV**, and **AAAI**.
